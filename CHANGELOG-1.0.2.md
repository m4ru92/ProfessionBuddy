# ProfessionBuddy 1.0.2

**New**

- **Gathering skill on tooltips:** hovering an ore vein, herb, or a skinnable / mineable / herbable creature now shows the required gathering skill, colored on the skill-up scale (orange → yellow → green → grey) against your current skill, alongside your own current skill level. Toggle under **Settings → Item Tooltips → Gather skill on tooltips** (on by default), with an option to show it for gathering professions you haven't learned.
- **Shift-click materials:** shift-click a material in a recipe's details panel (or the Material Calculator) to link it in chat, or drop it into the Auction House search box — the same way the recipe list on the left already works.

**Fixed**

- Crafted items that roll a random enchantment (e.g. Aquamarine Signet) now show the *<Random enchantment>* line in the recipe window; previously only their fixed stats appeared.
